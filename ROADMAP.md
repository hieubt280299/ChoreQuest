# ChoreQuest Roadmap

Ideas for future versions, to revise and pick from. Nothing here is promised. Once an idea is chosen, it gets its own `UPDATE_vX.Y.Z.md` spec and its status here changes to **Planned**, then **Done** (with the version).

**Size:** S = a few days · M = about a week · L = bigger, or needs a server.
**Status:** Idea · Planned · Done · Parked.

_Last revised: 2026-11-03 (after v1.7.2)._

---

## ⭐ Suggested next: v1.8.0

Together these make the game warmer between partners, give tickets and gold real-world meaning, and add daily variety. All three are S–M and need no server.

1. **Thank-you hearts** (S)
2. **Reward shop** (M)
3. **Lucky Quest of the Day** (S)

Then the **Weekly Boss Raid** as the headline of the version after.

## 🤝 Playing together

| Idea | Size | Status | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Weekly Boss Raid** | M | Idea | A pixel monster (Dust Dragon, Mold Slime…) appears each Monday with HP. Every quest either player does hits it. Beat it by Sunday to open a shared chest of tickets and gold. A weekly rhythm, and a reason to cheer each other on instead of only competing. |
| **Thank-you hearts** | S | Idea | Tap a heart on your partner's finished quest to say "Cảm ơn". They get a little bonus gold, and the Chronicle summary shows who was thanked most. |
| **Bounties** | M | Idea | Put some of your own gold on a chore ("+20 vàng nếu ai đổ rác tối nay"); whoever does it collects. Playful bargaining without real money. |
| **Nudge** | S | Idea | A gentle "poke" shown on your partner's Today card ("Anh nhắc em tưới cây nè"). Lighter than nagging. |

**To decide:**
- **Hearts:** a daily limit, so they stay meaningful?
- **Boss:** does the HP scale with how much the couple usually does?
- **Bounties:** what if nobody claims one? (The gold could go back to its owner at midnight.)

## 🎲 Something new each day

| Idea | Size | Status | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lucky Quest of the Day** | S | Idea | One random quest pays double gold today, highlighted on the Today card. |
| **Household events** | M | Idea | Rotating modifiers: "Rainy week: laundry +50% XP", "Guests are coming: cleaning pays more". Vietnamese seasons: a **Tết cleaning week** with double XP, lanterns in the cabin for **Trung thu**. |
| **Combos** | S | Idea | Finish 3 quests within an hour for a combo bonus. An "Early bird" badge for quests done before 9am. |

**To decide:**
- **Lucky Quest:** the same lucky quest for both players (fairer, and they can race for it)?
- **Events:** chosen by the moderator, or automatic on the calendar?

## 🏆 Long-term goals

| Idea | Size | Status | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Achievement book** | M | Idea | Pixel badges: "100 lần rửa bát", "Chuỗi 30 ngày", "Song kiếm hợp bích" (50 quests done together), plus a few hidden ones to discover. |
| **The cat grows up** | M | Idea | The cabin cat reflects how the home is doing: happy and playful in a busy week, sleepy when quests pile up, never punishing. Feed it treats with gold. |
| **Decorate the cabin** | L | Idea | Spend gold on furniture, plants and lights for the scene. Gives gold a use beyond the prize split, with a trade-off: decorate now, or keep your share. |
| **Chronicle reward track** | M | Idea | Each Chronicle gets a theme and a shared track of rewards (avatar frames, scene props, wheel skins) unlocked by combined XP. |

**To decide:**
- **Decorating:** gold spent on it would leave the prize pool. Is that a fair trade, or should decorations cost tickets?
- **Achievements:** shared by the couple, or one book each?

## 🎁 Real-life rewards

| Idea | Size | Status | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reward shop** | M | Idea | The couple writes their own rewards, such as "Massage 15 phút", "Được chọn phim tối nay" or "Ngủ nướng Chủ nhật", and buys them with tickets. Turning game currency into real treats is the strongest motivator here. |
| **Chronicle recap** | M | Idea | A "Wrapped"-style pixel card at the end of each Chronicle: "Bạn đã rửa 62 bộ bát", top chore, best streak. Easy to share on Zalo or Facebook, which also brings in new players. |

**To decide:**
- **Reward shop:** who sets the prices? Is there a confirmation that the reward was actually given?
- **Recap:** a shared image needs no account to view. Which details are fine to show publicly?

## 💛 Fairness and kindness

| Idea | Size | Status | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Streak freeze and rest days** | S | Idea | Spend a ticket to protect a streak, or pause everything during illness or travel. Losing a 20-day streak to a fever makes people quit. |
| **Balance meter** | S | Idea | A gentle weekly view of who did what ("Tuần này anh nấu nhiều hơn"), so an imbalance can be talked about without blame. |
| **Reminders** | L | Idea | An optional daily reminder notification. Needs push notifications and a small server; iPhones only support it for home-screen apps. |

**To decide:**
- **Rest days:** how many per Chronicle, and do they also pause the other player's streaks?
- **Reminders:** worth adding a server for (cost, upkeep), or keep the app serverless?

## 📝 Notes

- Keep the house rules: mobile first, short text, pixel icons (no emoji in the app), Vietnamese by default, and every new text in both languages.
- New currencies or rewards affect the balance of gold, XP and the prize pool. Check them against the payout before building.
- Add new ideas to the matching table with status **Idea**. Move dropped ones to **Parked** with a short reason, rather than deleting them.
